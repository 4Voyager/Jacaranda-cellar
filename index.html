01Our storyJacaranda Cellar
Our story

Wine, minus the wall of jargon.

Jacaranda Cellar began with a simple frustration: wonderful bottles were easy to find abroad and oddly hard to buy well in Nairobi. So we built the shop we wanted: a small, temperature-controlled cellar where every label has been tasted, argued over and priced honestly.

Whether it’s a Sunday nyama choma, a rooftop sundowner or a gift that has to land perfectly, our team will point you to the right bottle in plain language, within your budget, with zero pressure to buy.

  • 01

    Tasted, never guessed

    Every wine on our shelves is opened and tasted by our team before it earns its place.

  • 02

    Cellar-cool storage

    Held at a steady 14°C, so a bottle tastes the way its maker intended.

  • 03

    Delivered with care

    Packed to survive Nairobi’s roads and brought to your door properly chilled.
